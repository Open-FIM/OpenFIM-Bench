# SYN-019: T2D + resistant hypertension + CKD + gout

## Case summary

Mr. H is a 59-year-old man with type 2 diabetes, resistant hypertension, CKD stage 3a, gout, and obesity. His diet includes frequent salted soups, pickled vegetables, cured meats, sauces, white rice, sweetened beverages, and large evening meals. He has been advised to reduce sodium, reduce sugar, lose weight, and avoid gout triggers.

## Social and demographic context

Mr. H lives with his spouse, adult daughter, and father. He identifies as Vietnamese American. Family meals commonly include rice, noodle soups, fish sauce, soy sauce, pickled vegetables, broths, stir-fries, pork, seafood, and shared side dishes. He is not the primary cook and feels uncomfortable asking older family members to change traditional recipes.

He works in a warehouse and often eats late dinners. He has limited time for medical visits and does not track blood pressure consistently.

## Medical history

- Type 2 diabetes for 9 years
- Resistant hypertension
- CKD stage 3a with albuminuria
- Gout with several flares per year
- Obesity class I
- Hyperlipidemia

## Current medications

- Metformin
- SGLT2 inhibitor
- ACE inhibitor
- Thiazide-like diuretic
- Calcium channel blocker
- Allopurinol
- Statin

## Dietary pattern

Breakfast is often coffee and a pastry or leftover rice. Lunch is takeout noodles, rice bowls, or sandwiches. Dinner is a family meal with rice, soup, vegetables, fish or pork, and sauces. He drinks sweet tea several times per week. He uses fish sauce, soy sauce, bouillon, and pickled condiments daily. He occasionally uses a salt substitute purchased by a relative.

## Patient priorities

Mr. H wants to lower his blood pressure and prevent gout flares but does not want to reject family foods. He asks whether he must avoid seafood, beans, and meat entirely, and whether salt substitutes are safe.

## Additional case details

- He is willing to change portions and condiments.
- His spouse is open to lower-sodium cooking if flavor is preserved.
- He has not received culturally tailored sodium counseling.
- He does not know whether the thiazide medication affects gout.
- He prefers practical changes over a written meal plan.
