# SYN-014: T2D + low-carb LDL-C hyper-response + high ASCVD risk

## Case summary

Mr. K is a 58-year-old man with type 2 diabetes, obesity, hypertension, high coronary artery calcium, and strong family history of premature cardiovascular disease. He adopted a very-low-carbohydrate diet 5 months ago. His glucose, weight, and triglycerides improved, but LDL-C and ApoB rose markedly.

## Social and demographic context

Mr. K owns a small landscaping business and eats many meals on the road. He identifies as non-Hispanic White and describes himself as “anti-processed food.” He follows podcasts and online communities promoting ketogenic and carnivore-style diets. He distrusts mainstream nutrition advice because prior low-fat diets did not help him lose weight.

He lives with his spouse, who prefers a more balanced diet and is worried about his cholesterol. He has good insurance but rarely attends nutrition visits because he does not want to be told to “eat cereal and low-fat yogurt.”

## Medical history

- Type 2 diabetes for 6 years
- Obesity class I
- Hypertension
- High coronary artery calcium score
- Hyperlipidemia
- Family history of early myocardial infarction

## Current medications

- Metformin
- GLP-1 receptor agonist discussed but not started
- ARB
- Moderate-intensity statin
- Fish oil supplement purchased over the counter

## Recent clinical data

| Measure | Before low-carb | After 5 months low-carb |
|---|---:|---:|
| Weight | 222 lb | 198 lb |
| A1c | 8.1% | 6.7% |
| Triglycerides | 310 mg/dL | 125 mg/dL |
| LDL-C | 118 mg/dL | 198 mg/dL |
| ApoB | Elevated | Markedly elevated |
| HDL-C | 38 mg/dL | 51 mg/dL |
| Blood pressure | 144/86 mmHg | 132/78 mmHg |

## Dietary pattern

Breakfast is eggs cooked in butter with bacon or sausage. Lunch is bunless burgers, cheese, jerky, or deli meat. Dinner is steak, chicken thighs, pork, non-starchy vegetables, and salad with high-fat dressing. He uses butter, coconut oil, heavy cream, cheese, and low-carb packaged snacks. He avoids beans, fruit, oatmeal, whole grains, potatoes, and most starchy vegetables.

## Patient priorities

Mr. K feels that the diet “fixed” his diabetes and does not want to regain weight or see glucose rise. He asks whether LDL-C matters if his triglycerides and A1c improved. He is open to modifying the diet but does not want to return to a high-carbohydrate pattern.

## Additional case details

- He is adherent and motivated.
- He is skeptical of statin dose escalation.
- He is willing to eat fish, nuts, olive oil, and vegetables.
- He does not track saturated fat.
- He has not had ApoB explained to him in relation to ASCVD risk.
