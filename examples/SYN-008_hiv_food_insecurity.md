## Benchmark-relevant complexity
This case tests whether the system can distinguish ideal nutrition from feasible nutrition under severe food insecurity, avoid inappropriate assumptions about food choice and storage, account for medication interaction constraints, and prioritize practical risk reduction without blaming the patient for structural limitations.
